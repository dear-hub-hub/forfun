OUR APPROACH

From silicon to system,
with purpose.

We create integrated circuits for the interfaces where the physical and digital worlds meet. Our work spans high-precision sensing, data conversion, energy-efficient power management, and intelligent image systems.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

Analog and mixed-signal IC design
for intelligent sensing systems.

Precision IC design based on analog and mixed-signal circuits for sensors, wearable devices, intelligent robots, smart factories, measurement devices, IoT, AI, security, and automotive applications.

  • Analog & Mixed-Signal Circuits
  • Sensor Read-Out ICs
  • Ultra-High-Precision ICs
  • Instrumentation Amplifiers
  • Sub-Miniature ICs
  • Low-Power Analog Circuits
  • Analog-to-Digital Converters
  • Array Circuitry for IoT/AI
  • Power Management ICs
  • Neuron Circuit for Neuromorphic Computing (PiM)
